Overview

This calculator estimates the probability of rolling specific substats and substat values on an Echo in Wuthering Waves. It supports two distinct calculation modes: evaluating odds on a finished (+25) Echo across all 5 slots, or checking the odds of exact rolls so far (+5 to +25).

It does not account for main stat rolls, secondary stats (which are fixed by cost), or Sonata sets.

Calculation Modes

You can toggle the calculation mode at the top of the app to match your goal:

Mode What it answers How it works
Mode: Finished (+25)
(Default) 		"What are the odds my fully tuned Echo will contain these target stats?" Assumes a total of 5 unlocked slots. The target stats can appear in any of the 5 rolls, allowing filler/missed rolls in the remaining slots.
Mode: Exact Rolls "What were the odds of rolling strictly these stats across this exact number of rolls?" Assumes the Echo only has k slots unlocked (e.g. 2 rolls for a +10 Echo). Requires 100% of those rolls to hit your chosen attributes without filler stats.
Example: Selecting Crit Rate + Crit DMG (2 stats):
• Finished (+25): ~12.82% (roughly 1 in 8 echoes will have both somewhere in 5 slots).
• Exact Rolls: ~1.28% (roughly 1 in 78 echoes will hit both within strictly 2 rolls at +10).

What each Echo card shows

Every Echo card displays the following information:

Field Description
Cost 4, 3, or 1. Determines which main stats and secondary stats are available.
Main Stat Selected by the user. Substats are drawn from all 13 attributes independently of the main stat (duplicates of the main stat are allowed).
Secondary Stat Fixed by cost (ATK 150 for 4-cost, ATK 100 for 3-cost, HP 2280 for 1-cost).
Substats (up to 5) Each substat has an attribute and a rolled value. Both are included in the probability calculation.
Substats Roll Probability The chance of rolling the selected substat attributes based on the active calculation mode, regardless of their rolled values.
Roll Probability With Values The chance of rolling the selected substat attributes and their specific values together.

Substat attribute pool

There are 13 possible substat attributes in Wuthering Waves. Each roll chooses one attribute without replacement from the remaining pool:

Current substats on Echo Remaining pool size Chance of rolling one specific remaining attribute
0 substats (Roll 1) 13 1 / 13 (~7.6923%)
1 substat (Roll 2) 12 1 / 12 (~8.3333%)
2 substats (Roll 3) 11 1 / 11 (~9.0909%)
3 substats (Roll 4) 10 1 / 10 (10.0000%)
4 substats (Roll 5) 9 1 / 9 (~11.1111%)

We treat the final set of substats as unordered — the order in which they were rolled does not matter.

Substat value probability

After a substat attribute is selected, the game rolls a value for that attribute. Each attribute has a predefined distribution of 4 or 8 possible value tiers.

For example, Crit Rate can roll 6.30%, 6.90%, 7.50%, 8.10%, 8.70%, 9.30%, 9.90%, or 10.50%, each with official game-defined probabilities. The attribute roll probability is multiplied by the probability of rolling each specific value.

When the resulting roll probability is lower than 0.000001%, "Extremely low" is displayed to avoid cluttering the interface with long scientific notation.

Formulas

Let T = 13 be the total pool of attributes, k be the number of target substats selected, and N be the total number of slots considered:

1. Attribute Probability (Hypergeometric Distribution)

P(attributes) = Πi=0..k-1 (N - i) / (13 - i)
  • Mode: Finished (+25): Uses N = 5 (all slots on a fully tuned Echo).
    P = [5 × 4 × ... × (5 - k + 1)] / [13 × 12 × ... × (13 - k + 1)]
  • Mode: Exact Rolls: Uses N = k (strictly matching the number of selected slots).
    P = k! / [13 × 12 × ... × (13 - k + 1)] = 1 / C(13, k)

2. Roll Probability With Specific Values

P(attributes + values) = P(attributes) × Πj=1..k value_prob[j]

Where value_prob[j] is the individual probability of rolling that specific tier value for the j-th attribute.

Why there is no combined probability

Even a single Echo with several specific substats and values can have odds lower than 1 in 100,000. Multiplying multiple Echoes together across a 5-Echo set produces astronomical figures (e.g. 1 in 1025) that are mathematically true but not practically meaningful for gearing decisions. Each Echo card displays its own chances independently.
